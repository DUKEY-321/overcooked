## HostUtilities

`OC2DIYChefTrailColor` optionally depends on HostUtilities 1.8.0. Because the
HostUtilities release repository does not provide a redistributable license,
its DLL is not included. The installer can download the official Core archive
from the author’s release page and verifies its pinned SHA-256 before use.
